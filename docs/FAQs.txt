**How do I choose between the base-yield and rewards-yield tranches?**

* Picking your honey flavor is personal:

  + Base-yield tranche: For beras who like their rewards in the same farm they deposited (stick with $HONEY or $BERA)
  + Rewards-yield tranche: For adventurous bears who want to sample different forest flavors (other platform tokens and $f-BGT)

**Can I withdraw my funds at any time from BeraFarm vaults?**

* Ooga Booga! You betcha! Your honey is never locked in our cave - grab it whenever you need it.

**What benefits does $f-BGT offer compared to regular $BGT?**

* While regular $BGT is as attached to your address as honey to a bear's paw, $f-BGT breaks free:

  + Roams the DeFi forest freely while still being backed 1:1 by real $BGT
  + Mingles with other DeFi protocols like a social bear
  + Can still be burnt and exchanged for $BERA tokens when you're feeling hungry
  + Finds extra honey pots through specialized $f-BGT strategies

**What is the "fBGT Controller" mentioned in the whitepaper?**

* Think of the "fBGT Controller" as the master beekeeper of our hive. It's where all the $BGT earned from our DeFi honey hunts gets stored. This clever bera crafts the liquid $f-BGT tokens (backed 1:1 by real $BGT), essentially building a bridge between the soulbound tokens stuck in their cave and the free-roaming tokens exploring the forest.

**How does BeraFarm maximize my yields?**

* While you're hibernating, our protocol is doing the heavy lifting:

  + Sniffing out the sweetest honey spots across the DeFi landscape
  + Dancing with top validators to secure the juiciest delegation rewards

  + Automatically reinvesting your earnings faster than a bera can climb a tree

**What happens if I sleep through a governance vote?**

* Not a problem, sleepyhead! Our protocol never hibernates - we're actively participating in all important votes to maximize your rewards while you enjoy your beauty rest.

**Is my $BGT safe in BeraFarm?**

* Safer than honey in a bear's den! Our protocol is battle-tested, and guarded by the most vigilant bears in the forest.
