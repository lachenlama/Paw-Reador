Multi-strategy vaults
=====================

> *Diversification at It's Best*

---

![](https://berafarm.gitbook.io/~gitbook/image?url=https%3A%2F%2F3288066147-files.gitbook.io%2F%7E%2Ffiles%2Fv0%2Fb%2Fgitbook-x-prod.appspot.com%2Fo%2Fspaces%252FOvo3UFlmhHFt4nhY532w%252Fuploads%252FJyRA74BZ04pJ20b2t4bX%252FBerafarm%2520Architecture.png%3Falt%3Dmedia%26token%3D9dae58f5-a351-4953-830d-037398296c69&width=768&dpr=4&quality=100&sign=a4629213&sv=2)

Each vault serves as the entry point for managing user deposits and withdrawals. When funds enter a vault, they're automatically forwarded to the strategy manager, which intelligently distributes these assets across multiple high-performing strategies based on predefined allocations.

The vault's dynamic allocation system routes funds to diverse DeFi strategies, such as liquidity provision on DEXs - BEX, Kodiaq, lending on Dolomite, and other synthetic strategies. Our proprietary strategy rebalancer continuously monitors performance metrics and market conditions, optimizing strategy allocations to maximize returns while maintaining appropriate risk parameters.

---

### **Example Deposit Flow:**

1. User deposits 1,000 $HONEY into BeraFarm's $HONEY vault
2. The strategy manager receives the 1,000 $HONEY and distributes it:

   * 500 $HONEY (50%) to BEX liquidity pools
   * 300 $HONEY (30%) to Dolomite lending
   * 200 $HONEY (20%) to other optimized strategies
3. As market conditions change, the rebalancer might adjust these allocations (e.g., shifting more to Dolomite if lending rates increase)
4. All yields generated from these strategies flow back to the vault and are distributed according to the user's farm selection (Base-Yield or Rewards-Yield)

---

This multi-strategy approach ensures capital efficiency and risk diversification while requiring zero management from users.
