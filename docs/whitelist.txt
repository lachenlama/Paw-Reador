## 📋 Available Commands

### 🔗 Register Your Wallet


Use this command /wallet_register to **register your wallet** for whitelist access to the BeraFarm Beta launch.  
Make sure your address is **bear-fect**!

---

### ✅ Check Your Status

Want to see if you're all set?  
This command /wallet_status will show your **registered wallet status** – no need to bear any doubts!

---

### ❌ Deregister Your Wallet

Need to un-register?  
Use this command /wallet_deregister if you wish to **remove your current wallet registration**.

---

### 🔄 Update Your Address

Already registered but want to change wallets?  
**No prob-bear-m!**

Simply use: 

/wallet_register [new address]

It will **automatically override** your previous wallet address.
